Privacy Policy

Rollumi: Color Maze · Last updated: 22 October 2026.

1. Controller and contact

KCreations
Email: lemnolv@gmail.com

This notice covers the Android game Rollumi: Color Maze, internally named SwipeMaze, package com.KCreations.SwipeMaze.

2. Playing and local information

The game does not require a separate game account. Progress, stars, best move counts, collected items, Coins, unlocked patterns and paint colors, purchase entitlements and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate a dedicated cloud game-save service. Device backups may be provided separately by your operating system.

The game does not use Unity Analytics for gameplay tracking. Progress, best move counts and other statistics needed for the game are stored locally for its features.

Advertising, purchases and support involve the additional processing described below.

3. Rewarded advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements. Google's advertising services can process an IP address and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad-related interactions such as video views and taps, and diagnostic or performance information. These services use data to deliver and measure ads and help prevent abuse. Available identifiers depend on the device and privacy settings.

Choosing whether to watch a rewarded ad is separate from choices about personal-data processing. Technical data may also be processed when the advertising service initializes or loads an ad. Test advertisements do not mean that no data is processed.

Current test configuration: the test version uses test advertisements. Advertising consent is still being configured and the current test build does not display an advertising-consent form. This configuration and notice will be updated before the public release.

Where an advertising consent or privacy-options form is available, it explains the choices for the relevant region. Device advertising controls and Google's ad settings provide additional options. These controls do not erase all information already processed.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from services that use its advertising products.

4. Purchases and service diagnostics

Purchases are processed through Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing. Purchase information, receipts or tokens and technical identifiers are used to deliver paid content, verify entitlements and handle purchase problems. The game does not receive full payment-card or bank details from your Google Play payment.

Unity IAP can process installation and session identifiers, available player identifiers, device information and country, purchase records, and crash or performance information for its purchase, diagnostic and related service-analysis functions. We do not request an email address for a game account or operate a separate webshop.

Google and Unity also process data under their applicable notices and service terms. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity IAP privacy information.

5. Feedback and support

The feedback button opens your email application with an editable message containing the app version, platform, device model and operating system. The game does not send that message itself. If you send it, the recipient receives your sender address, message and the information you include. The feedback recipient shown in your email application may differ from the privacy contact above.

We use correspondence to answer requests, investigate problems and handle purchase or privacy questions. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Where personal data is necessary to provide requested game functions or fulfill a purchase, we rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Necessary legal recordkeeping and responses to statutory requests rely on Article 6(1)(c). Other support correspondence, service security and prevention of fraud can rely on Article 6(1)(f), where our interest in responding and keeping the service secure is not overridden by your rights.

Consent under Article 6(1)(a), together with applicable rules on access to device information, is required where the relevant advertising processing requires permission. Watching an ad is not itself a substitute for that permission. The current test advertising configuration is described in section 3. Providers acting as independent controllers explain their own purposes and bases in their privacy notices.

7. Recipients and international processing

Depending on the feature used, data is processed by Google, Unity and their service providers. Our email provider processes correspondence you send. We do not operate a separate server for your gameplay progress or level-analytics events.

Processing may take place outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. The applicable arrangements and safeguards depend on the provider and processing activity. Their linked privacy notices describe international processing and available safeguards. You can contact us for information about safeguards applicable to processing for which we are responsible.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data: normally remains until you clear the app's storage or otherwise remove it. Separate device backups may remain. Clearing data can permanently remove progress, Coins and consumable purchases.
  • Purchases and provider records: retention depends on fulfillment, entitlements, security and legal obligations. Unity's IAP documentation describes purchase records as retained until deletion and service logs for 90 days; other providers and required legal records can have different periods.
  • Support: correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request, meet applicable legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims, then deleted when those purposes no longer apply.

Request data deletion

Email lemnolv@gmail.com with the subject Rollumi data deletion request. Describe the records you want deleted. For a purchase-related request, an order reference and approximate date can help us locate the records. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

We may ask for limited information needed to verify your request and locate the records. We delete data for which we are responsible where the conditions for erasure are met and coordinate relevant requests to providers processing data on our behalf. For a provider's independent processing, a direct request to that provider may be necessary. Information required for legal obligations, fraud prevention or legal claims may be retained. We normally respond to GDPR requests within one month and will explain any legally permitted extension.

To remove local game progress and settings, use Android's App settings → Rollumi: Color Maze → Storage → Clear storage/data; wording may differ by device. This can permanently remove local progress and consumable items. It does not erase provider records, cancel purchases or remove separate device backups.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for the future without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Contact lemnolv@gmail.com. We may need limited additional information to verify a request and locate the relevant records. We do not link all installation data to an email address. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

10. Intended audience and updates

The intended audience is players aged 16 and over. The game is not directed at children under 16. Parents or guardians with concerns about a child's information can contact us.

We will update this notice when our data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
